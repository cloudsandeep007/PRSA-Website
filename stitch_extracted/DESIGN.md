## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the tension between razor-sharp athletic precision and raw, explosive kinetic energy. Drawing inspiration from high-performance aerospace telemetry, elite Olympic training compounds, and the adrenaline of extreme motorsports, the interface feels less like an administrative portal and more like a tactical cockpit for human flight.

The aesthetic fuses **Precision High-Contrast Minimalism** with **Atmospheric Luminescence**. Deep void-black canvases provide an absolute baseline, pierced by razor-thin vector accents, crystalline ice-refractions, and high-frequency chromatic glows. The experience evokes absolute mastery, uncompromising luxury, and relentless forward momentum. The target audience—elite youth prospects, Olympic contenders, and discerning high-net-worth patrons—demands a visual standard that mirrors five-figure training programs: sleek, authoritative, frictionless, and unmistakably bespoke.

## Colors

The palette operates under an uncompromising dark mode paradigm where light is treated as speed, acceleration, and precision.

- **Primary Canvas & Depth:** 
  - Absolute Obsidian (`#090A0F`): Deepest background layer and viewport boundary.
  - Sub-Zero Charcoal (`#0E1118`): Primary surface elevation for cards, modals, and navigation bars.
  - Cryo Slate (`#141824`): Active hover states, structural partitions, and recessed component containers.
- **Accents & Luminescence:**
  - Electric Hyper-Cyan (`#00F0FF`): Denotes pure velocity, blade sharpness, active telemetry, and primary focal interaction points.
  - Solar Crimson (`#FF462D`): Reserved strictly for high-impact calls to action, tournament countdowns, urgency markers, and personal best records.
  - Ion Blue (`#2563EB`): The structural kinetic spine; used for deep glow diffusion, active secondary controls, and data streams.
- **Typographic & Interface Neutrals:**
  - Glacial White (`#FFFFFF`): Primary headings and essential data points.
  - Platinum Silver (`#F1F5F9`): Body copy and high-legibility secondary values.
  - Tactical Muted Slate (`#94A3B8`): Metadata, captions, deactivated boundaries, and inactive controls.

## Typography

The typographical pairing sets up a dialogue between technological tension and organic legibility.

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`):** Chosen for its technical, kinetic geometry. Monospaced undertones and architectural terminals reflect physical ice friction and telemetry readouts. Headlines should utilize tight letter spacing and maintain aggressive vertical cadence. All category markers, badges, and sub-labels must use `label-uppercase` styled in forced uppercase with expanded tracking (`+0.14em`).
- **Body & Longform (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Chosen for supreme legibility at high speed and low contrast. Its geometric balance maintains clarity against dark glass surfaces without fatiguing the reader.
- **Telemetry Readouts:** For performance metrics (lap times, edge velocity, heart rate), use `metric-stat` styled with tabular numbers to prevent horizontal jitter during real-time data streaming.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in a 12-column kinetic fluid grid structured around 4px / 8px atomic increments.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, `margin` of 3rem (48px), `gutter` of 1.5rem (24px). Max container width is fixed at 1440px with dynamic exterior margins.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin` of 2rem (32px), `gutter` of 1.25rem (20px).
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` of 1.25rem (20px), `gutter-mobile` of 0.75rem (12px).
- **Rhythm & Spatial Composition:**
  - High-density telemetry blocks utilize tight padding (`space-sm` to `space-md`) paired with razor borders to replicate cockpit instruments.
  - Hero sections and coach showcase stages breathe via dramatic vertical expanses (`space-2xl`), accentuating prestige and exclusivity through generous negative space.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical transparency, chromatic light diffusion, and precision edge lighting rather than standard blur dropshadows.

- **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Flat `#090A0F`. No blur, no elevation.
- **Level 1 (Frosted Deck - Base Cards & Bars):** Background `#0E1118` at 85% opacity with an active `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`. Border is a crisp 1px gradient stroke: `#FFFFFF` at 12% alpha at the top edge fading to `#FFFFFF` at 3% alpha at the bottom.
- **Level 2 (Active Interactive Elements & Overlays):** Background `#141824` at 92% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`. Ambient shadow: `0 16px 36px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.75)`. Rim lighting: top-edge 1px stroke of `rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.35)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Dialogs & Floating Tactical HUDs):** Solid elevated obsidian `#0E1118` wrapped in an external laser rim glow: `0 0 24px 0 rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.15), 0 32px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.9)`.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture embraces an intentional **Soft Precision** standard (`roundedness: 1`). 

- Corners are disciplined and crisp (base radius of 0.25rem / 4px; medium elements 0.5rem / 8px). This creates an aerodynamic silhouette reminiscent of freshly carved ice blades, carbon fiber skates, and telemetry frames.
- Pills and maximum roundings are reserved solely for high-visibility badges, status tags, and specific quick-tap athletic CTA buttons to create an intentional focal contrast against the angular architecture of the grid.

## Components

### Buttons & Kinetic CTAs
- **Primary Velocity CTA:** Angled or pill-shaped container filled with solid Electric Hyper-Cyan (`#00F0FF`). Label in deep `#090A0F` utilizing `label-uppercase` in bold. Hover triggers a radial cyan bloom (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 240, 255, 0.5)`) and micro-shifts forward by 1px.
- **High-Stakes / Urgency CTA:** Solid Solar Crimson (`#FF462D`) with Glacial White text. Used solely for "Book Elite Evaluation", "Register for Nationals", and direct trial triggers. Hover adds an intense amber-crimson aura.
- **Ghost Tactical Button:** Transparent fill, 1px perimeter border in Platinum Silver (`#F1F5F9`) at 25% opacity. Text in Glacial White. Hover state raises border alpha to 90% and introduces a subtle `#141824` backdrop fill.

### Cards & Surfaces
- **Frosted Glass Cards:** Tinted with `#0E1118` at 80% opacity with backdrop blur. Top edge highlighted with a 1px pseudo-gradient line (`rgba(255,255,255,0.15)` to `transparent`).
- **Coach Spotlight Cards:** Full-height imagery of the master coach or athlete treated with high-contrast duotone shadows. Overlaid with an integrated glassmorphic telemetry footer featuring dynamic performance badges, Olympic credentials, and win ratios.

### Performance Badges & Chips
- **Telemetry Chips:** Pill-shaped, micro-height (24px), padded with `space-xs` and `space-sm`. Dark semi-transparent base (`#0E1118` at 60%), with active status beacons: a pulsing 6px dot in Cyan (`#00F0FF`) or Solar Crimson (`#FF462D`). Text in `label-uppercase`.

### Input Controls & Trial Booking Fields
- **Tactical Text Inputs:** Crisp, flat `#0E1118` backgrounds with a subtle inset shadow. Default border is 1px slate (`rgba(148, 163, 184, 0.2)`). On focus, border snaps instantly to Electric Cyan (`#00F0FF`) backed by an inner 0.5px bloom. Field labels hover above inputs in `label-uppercase` muted slate.
- **Interactive Segmented Tabs:** Deep recessed tray (`#090A0F`) with sliding active pill indicators that snap into place with ease-out cubic transitions. Active tab is frosted `#141824` with razor-sharp cyan top-edge accents.

### Statistical Counters
- High-impact layout featuring `metric-stat` figures accented by miniature trend arrows in Hyper-Cyan or Solar Crimson. Sub-labels anchored below in muted slate to ground the quantitative visual hierarchy.
