ABOUT PRSA BANGALORE

BUILDING ATHLETES.

Professional Roller Skating Academy (PRSA) operates specialized training facilities across South Bengaluru, including Electronic City, Neo Town, and HSR Layout corridors. Officially affiliated with the Roller Skating Federation of India (RSFI), we merge safety-first grassroots foundation with elite state & national speed racing.

From toddlers mastering balance on 4-wheel quad skates to championship racers tucking into high-velocity 110mm inline aerodynamic road sprints, our dedicated floodlit skating track provides the safest, most supportive arena in Karnataka.

QUESTIONS ANSWERED

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Do we need our own roller skates for the free trial session? expand_more

No! For your free trial class, PRSA provides certified quad or inline skates, certified helmets, knee pads, and elbow guards completely free. If you choose to enroll, our coaches perform a foot shape audit to advise on the right wheels and boot specifications.

Should my child start on Quad Skates or Inline Speed Skates? expand_more

For young kids (ages 4 to 6), quad skates provide broader lateral balance and help overcome fear of falling. Skaters age 7 and up or those with prior balance experience can choose either Quads or Inlines based on their competition interest. Our coaches assess this on Day 1.

Is PRSA officially affiliated with RSFI? expand_more

Yes, PRSA is fully aligned with Roller Skating Federation of India (RSFI) racing guidelines. Our competitive skaters receive official RSFI athlete registration cards and represent the academy at district, state, and national championship fixtures.

Can adults join without any previous roller skating experience? expand_more

Absolutely. Our Adult Fitness & Open Rink program is tailored for working adults seeking low-impact cardiovascular conditioning. We begin with basic rink glides, core posture, and stopping techniques in a welcoming, mature environment.
